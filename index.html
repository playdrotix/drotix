FAQ

Quick answers

Is Tic Tac Toe free?

The game is free to download and play. [Update this answer if you add in-app purchases or ads.]

Which devices does it run on?

Android and iOS phones and tablets. Store links will appear on this site when the app is live.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play against The Doll and in Pass & Play as a guest. An account is needed for online matches, leaderboards, friends, tournaments and to keep your coins and progress on a new phone.

How do coins work?

Coins are virtual game currency. You earn them by winning, finishing missions and claiming daily rewards, and you can spend them on random-match stakes, tournament entry and cosmetics. Coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Can I win real money?

No. There are no cash prizes and no real-money gambling. Everything is for fun and bragging rights.
